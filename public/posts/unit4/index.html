UNIT 4

This unit consisted of two separate projects where the first involved us creating a dashboard on Metabase and the other task was to create a chatbot.

The Metabase project was challenging in primarily two different aspects: writing SQL queries and figuring out what and how we as a group can present ‌multiple aspects that can be visualized using the data set provided and still make a cohesive story. To find out what each of the dashboards should represent we as a team did some research on what types of charts can be made using the data in the set and then thought of ways to connect different charts to our individual topics and later to make a cohesive group of dashboards. I focused on finding ways of showing the capabilities and limitations of the data set. I am already familiar with Metabase since I have used it before ‌coming to university and I always enjoy using it whenever I have the opportunity.

For the Typebot project I went with the option of making a bot that would help students who are considering joining the university. This turned out to be much more complex than expected. I first drew out a mind map of all the things I wanted to add to the bot and then started building using Typebot. The core challenge wasn’t making the bot since Typebot is very intuitive to use, but it was thinking about how to organize and sequence the information for specific audiences.
